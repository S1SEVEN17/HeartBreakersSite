🔑 Your Limited API key

Make one in Torn → Settings → API Keys → Create key → Limited Access, named e.g. "Heart Breakers Hub". It's checked with Torn (it must be yours), then stored encrypted - nobody, leaders included, can ever see it.

📤 What to share

💪 Battle statsStrength, defense, speed, dexterity - saved daily so your gains are tracked
🧰 Work statsManual labor, intelligence, endurance - saved daily
⚔️ War readinessLeaders see your energy, happy, drug / booster / medical cooldowns, refill and status - live, for war planning

📜 How your key and data are used

Data storagePersistent - until you delete it here, turn everything off, leave Heart Breakers, or your key stops existing
Data sharingYour choice per item: faction leaders only, or faction members. War readiness: leaders only. Never outside the faction
Purpose of useTracking your battle and work stat gains, and war readiness for faction leaders' war planning
Key storage & sharingStored remotely, encrypted · used only for automation (a daily stats reading, and live readiness when a leader opens it) · never shared or shown
Key access levelLimited Access
  • Delete any time: "Delete my key and all my data" removes your key at once and your saved stats with the next update.
  • Leaving the faction: your key and all your saved stats are deleted automatically.
  • Deleting your key or Torn account: when Torn reports the key no longer exists, everything is deleted automatically.
  • Turning one item off deletes that item's saved history. Every request is made with the comment "Heart Breakers Hub", so you can see it in Torn's API key log.
