📤 What to share

Make one in Torn → Settings → API Keys → Create key → Limited Access, named e.g. "Heart Breakers Hub". It's checked with Torn (it must be yours), then stored on the Hub, encrypted so your data can be read while you're away - nobody, leaders included, can ever see it.
💪 Battle statsStrength, defense, speed, dexterity - saved daily so your gains are tracked
🧰 Work statsManual labor, intelligence, endurance - saved daily
⚔️ War readinessLeaders see your energy, happy, drug / booster / medical cooldowns, refill and status - live, for war planning

⛓️ Chain timer (during live chains)Lets the Hub read the faction chain with your stored key during a live chain, only when your own Chain Watch script isn't connected, to keep the shared timer alive. Chain reads only; never your personal data; at most ~2 calls/min.

📜 How your key and data are used

Key storage🔒 Stored on the Hub (encrypted)
Used only for automation (battle / work stats once a day at 00:00 TCT, OC pass rates whenever the Hub finds a new crime, war readiness once a minute only while a leader has the War readiness page open, and - if you turn on the chain timer - the faction chain at most ~2 times a minute during a live chain while your own Chain Watch script isn't connected) · never shared or shown
Key access levelLimited Access
Data storagePersistent - until you delete it here, turn everything off, leave Heart Breakers, or your key stops existing
Data sharingYour choice per item: faction leaders only, or faction members. War readiness: leaders only. OC pass rates: faction leaders (OC Management) and faction members using the OC Optimizer script (its role suggestions show your name and rate). Never outside the faction
Purpose of useTracking your battle and work stat gains, war readiness for faction leaders' war planning, building organized crime teams with the best pass rate in every role, and keeping the faction's shared chain timer accurate (chain timer: faction chain only - nothing about you is read or saved)
  • Delete any time: "Delete my key and all my data" removes your key at once and your saved stats with the next update.
  • Leaving the faction: your key and all your saved stats are deleted automatically.
  • Deleting your key or Torn account: when Torn reports the key no longer exists, everything is deleted automatically.
  • Turning one item off deletes that item's saved history. Every request is made with the comment "Heart Breakers Hub", so you can see it in Torn's API key log.
